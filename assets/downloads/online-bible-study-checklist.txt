DHL ONLINE BIBLE STUDY HOST CHECKLIST
(Sample template from DHL – Diaspora Hub for Leaders. Edit freely.)

THE DAY BEFORE
[ ] Send the meeting link and the Bible passage to the group
[ ] Prepare 3-5 discussion questions
[ ] Ask someone to open in prayer
[ ] Choose a co-host to welcome newcomers and watch the chat

15 MINUTES BEFORE
[ ] Open the meeting and test audio and screen sharing
[ ] Put the passage reference in the chat

DURING (about 60 minutes)
[ ] Welcome everyone by name; introduce newcomers (5 min)
[ ] Opening prayer (3 min)
[ ] Read the passage aloud, in more than one language if helpful (5 min)
[ ] Discussion in small breakout rooms of 3-4 people (25 min)
[ ] Share one thing each group learned (10 min)
[ ] Prayer requests and closing prayer (10 min)
[ ] Announce the next meeting and one next step

AFTER
[ ] Message newcomers personally within 24 hours
[ ] Share prayer requests only with permission
[ ] Invite regular attendees to meet in person when possible
    (Online is the bridge, not the destination.)
