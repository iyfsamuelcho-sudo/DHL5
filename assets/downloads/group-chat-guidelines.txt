(Sample template from DHL – Diaspora Hub for Leaders. Edit freely.)

Welcome! This group is a place to encourage one another, share prayer
requests and help each other with life in Korea.

1. Be kind and respectful. We come from many countries and churches.
2. Stay on topic: faith, encouragement, prayer and practical help.
3. No selling, advertising or chain messages.
4. Protect privacy. Don't share someone's personal story, photo or
   contact details without their permission.
5. Ask before adding new members, and introduce them when you do.
6. Share prayer requests freely; share other people's requests only
   with their permission.
7. Disagree gently, and take long debates to a private conversation.
8. If you see something harmful, message a moderator.
