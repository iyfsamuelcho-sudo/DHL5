    Follow DHL

      Welcome to DHL

      Moving to Korea means a new language, a new job, new rules and, often, loneliness. DHL is a place to find practical help and real friends, and to explore faith at your own pace.

      We're a digital ministry hub for foreigners in Korea, especially Filipinos. We gather people who find us online, connect them with a community, and walk with them as they grow, from their first question to one day leading others.
