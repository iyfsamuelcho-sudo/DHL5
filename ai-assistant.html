DHL AI Ministry Assistant

Ask a question and get an answer from DHL's resources, with sources you can check. For personal or difficult questions, talk to a mentor.

How it works

  1. You ask a question, in English, Filipino, Taglish or Korean.
  2. DHL AI checks what the Bible itself says first, with its context.
  3. Where it helps, it explains the interpretation or theological connection.
  4. When you ask about Pastor Ock Soo Park's teaching, it explains his teaching separately, from his books.
  5. Sources and Bible references are shown so you can read them yourself.
  6. If it isn't sure, or the question is personal, it recommends a DHL mentor.

DHL AI is an educational assistant. It does not replace personal Bible study, pastoral guidance, or a human mentor.

This version runs in demo mode: it searches DHL study notes on this website and shows the best match. No AI service is connected yet, and your questions are not stored.

Two sources, kept separate

DHL AI always tells you which source an answer comes from.

  • The Holy Bible

    The primary source. Answers start from what the text says, in its historical and literary context, with cross-references from Genesis to Revelation.

    You'll see phrases like “In the Bible…” and “In this passage…”

  • Rev. Ock Soo Park's books

    Teaching resources that explain Scripture, including The Secret of Forgiveness of Sin and Being Born Again, Who Are You Who Is Dragging Me? and teachings on the Tabernacle.

    You'll see phrases like “Pastor Ock Soo Park explains this as…”

Pastor Park's teaching is never presented as a quotation from the Bible. If the exact text of a book isn't in DHL's knowledge base, the assistant says so rather than guessing. The books are copyrighted, so the assistant gives summaries, explanations and short quotations, never whole chapters.

DHL knowledge base

The live assistant will draw only on materials DHL has approved and has permission to use. It retrieves relevant passages rather than reproducing whole books.
