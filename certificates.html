How it works

  1. Study the lessonsRead each lesson and the Bible passages, then mark it complete.
  2. Pass the quizScore 80% or more. You can try as many times as you need.
  3. Get your certificateEnter your name and print it or save it as a PDF.

DHL certificates recognize completion of a self-paced DHL course. They are not government-accredited degrees or formal academic qualifications. For mentored leadership training, see the Leadership Academy.
